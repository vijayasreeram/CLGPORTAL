What makes a strong fresher resume

  • Start with a short summary focused on software, analytics, AI, or your target role.
  • Show your strongest academic metrics like CGPA, semester, and department clearly.
  • Add projects with impact, tools used, and outcomes instead of generic descriptions.
  • Keep coding profiles, certificates, and hackathons relevant to the job role.
  • Use simple black text, strong alignment, and clean spacing for recruiter readability.
